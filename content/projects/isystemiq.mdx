## The Problem

Navigating US university admissions, visa guidance, and Day 1 CPT education is a genuinely confusing process for students coming from India — a lot of scattered, inconsistent information and not enough trustworthy, professional guidance in one place. A consultancy built to help with exactly that needed a website that reflected the same professionalism and clarity it offers students directly.

## The Solution

iSystemIQ is a modern, responsive marketing and lead-generation website for an education consultancy: clear information architecture around admissions, visa guidance, and Day 1 CPT education, contact forms built to actually convert an interested visitor into a lead, and a production deployment built to represent the practice professionally from day one.

## Key Highlights

- Modern responsive website
- Lead generation
- Contact forms
- SEO optimized
- Professional branding
- Mobile responsive
- Production deployment

## Technical Approach

Built as a modern, responsive marketing site with production deployment, SEO, and mobile responsiveness treated as requirements from the start rather than an afterthought — a professional consultancy's website is often a prospective student's first impression of the practice, so performance and clarity mattered as much as the content itself.
